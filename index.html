People, Place, Possibility

Our approach reflects our passion. In practice it means three things for a client.

People

Engineering is done with people, not to them. You work directly with the principal engineer, get straight answers, and know who is accountable for the work from concept through construction.

Place

We live and work here. Our practice is grounded in Colorado City, Hildale and Centennial Park, in northern Mohave County, Arizona and southern Utah, and we also work in southern Nevada, including Mesquite. We know the ground, the agencies and the constraints, and that shows up in the design.

Possibility

Good engineering opens options. We look for the practical path that makes a site, a subdivision or a utility extension workable — technically sound, permittable and constructible on a real budget and schedule.

How we work

Better Together

Complex projects succeed through coordination — among owners, agencies, utilities, designers, contractors and communities. Excel Design Associates is principal-led: the engineer who plans the work is the one who does it, stands behind it, and coordinates the people and decisions needed to move it forward. We pair that direct involvement with long-standing local knowledge of the region and its jurisdictions, so the path through permitting and construction is clear and defensible before the first drawing is issued.
